## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** 7/10 — genuinely product-grounded. Radar rings render the actual route scan; telemetry is honest instrument language. But the skeleton is the standard censorship-app trope set, and the "one ember" discipline leaks at the margins: a filled-amber "Balanced" preset pill sits inches from a gray ignition disc.

**Deterministic scan:** 18 findings across `src/components` + `src/log-window.ts` — 15 `design-system-font-size` (6 genuine off-ramp drift: 11px & 19px IpDisplay/StatusIndicator/ProxyIndicator/SidecarErrorScreen/button 0.8rem; 9 rejected as documented chip sizes) and 3 in log-window.ts:78 — **indigo `#c7d2fe` + `rgba(99,102,241,0.3)` search highlight and a 2px radius**, all genuine violations of DESIGN.md's palette/radius rules. Detector caught log-window indigo and the 19px IP display that review missed. Detector quirk: `design-system-*` rules stamp severity advisory but lack the advisory flag, forcing exit 2.

**Visual overlays:** injection into the live-built page succeeded and the detector ran clean — but the app renders a blank page outside the Tauri runtime (`window.__TAURI_INTERNALS__` undefined crashes before React mounts), so no reliable overlay evidence; the page-crash itself is a real finding for any pure-web preview/debug workflow.

## Overall Impression

A disciplined, honest instrument that deserves its trust — and wastes it in the two moments that matter: the leak warning is the quietest element on screen, and connection errors speak raw Rust to a non-technical user. Biggest opportunity: make the emergency states as loud as the calm states are quiet.

## What's Working

1. **State honesty as reassurance** — every status is a real fact (TCP probe, parsed scan budget, IP comparison), triple-coded icon+color+motion+text. Perfect for a paranoid user.
2. **Rock-stable signature hero** — fixed `h-60` geometry, 160px disc, phasic rings/spinner/check/shake, focus ring, scoped aria-live.
3. **Rare interaction discipline** — focus-visible rings everywhere, reduced-motion honored, focus-gated compositor loops, double-confirmed destructive actions.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P0] Leak detection is invisible and unactionable** — PublicLocation.tsx:38-43: 10px ShieldAlert inside a 260px pill, no motion, no "Disconnect now", no guidance. **Why:** a leak is existential for this persona; the app's loudest-emotion moment is its quietest render. **Fix:** full-surface red banner/overlay with shield icon, motion, "Disconnect now", re-check. **Command:** `/impeccable harden`

2. **[P1] Error/reconnect states fail to recover the user** — ConnectionStatusLine branded raw mono error strings; "Attempt 2 of 3" without reassurance; no next step, no escalate-to-Stealth route (the one a strict network needs) outside a 5-option toggle in a collapsed accordion. **Fix:** human, translatable summary + action (deep-link to Stealth via existing `openAdvancedHighlightScan`), technical detail behind Full Log. **Command:** `/impeccable clarify`

3. **[P1] Idle overload + hierarchy inversion** — two pixel-identical unlabeled pill rows (9 buttons), filled-amber preset under a gray disc, "More options" chevron glyph; 8 decision points at/over the ≤4 ceiling (Scan Mode 5, QuickConnect 5, color picker 20, auto-rotate 7). **Fix:** one labeled quick row; active chips as hairline+amber-dot not filled ember; collapse Ironclad into an on-error "escalate" affordance. **Command:** `/impeccable distill`

4. **[P2] Jargon at the surface for the first-timer** — MASQUE / WARP-in-WARP / Zero Trust / SOCKS5 hover-only (after 300ms); Persian users get no inline meaning; first-run notification ask lands before the first connect. **Fix:** one-line sub-captions on the quick row; keep tooltips for depth. **Command:** `/impeccable clarify`

5. **[P2] Ember discipline drift (confirmed by detector)** — indigo search highlight in log-window.ts:78, 19px/11px off-ramp sizes, six `text-primary` section labels per open panel, "Secure" mislabel (QuickConnect.tsx:54), Banner close X with no accessible name, light-theme swatch ring invisible. **Fix:** one batch pass; fix ACTIVE_PRESET map, unlabel, sizes to ramp. **Command:** `/impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user):** single global shortcut (Ctrl+Shift+C) discoverable only via aria-label, with a muscle-memory collision (browser "copy plain text" chord); history rows are inert — no way to re-apply last session's exact settings.

**Jordan (first-timer — the real primary user):** 5-second connect test passes; everything else fails — two identical pill rows, jargon with hover-only meaning, first-run permission ask before the first connect, a permanent "Location unavailable" whisper-pill on idle (guaranteed on censored networks), and the escalation path for failure routed through Advanced.

**Sam (accessibility):** strong base (rings, labels, reduced-motion). Breaks: Banner close X unnamed; ColorTheme selected-ring invisible in light theme; 9-10px type at 70-80% opacity pushes AA limits; `tracking-widest` uppercase Latin labels will corrupt Persian glyphs if copy lands — needs `:lang(fa)` off-switch.

## Minor Observations

- Duplicate timers: TitleBar uptime + status-line elapsed on one screen

## Questions to Consider

1. If the disc is the product, why is the loudest idle element a *preset switch* next to a gray ignition disc — whom is the filled active-state really for?
2. Would the first-timer ever be hurt if the surface said "Faster / Sneakiest / Guaranteed" and protocol names moved into tooltips?
3. Why is the leak state — the app's highest-trust event — rendered quieter than its own re-check button?
4. Is "progressive disclosure" real here, or is it "one drawer holds everything, two identical drawers beside it"?
